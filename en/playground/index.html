Interactive Installation | 2022

I led the visual design and interaction flow, building it together with an engineering partner.

About

PC Game | 2021

Fowl Justice

Project Overview

A 2D vertical scrolling action game with four levels. The player fights as a turtledove warrior to defend their home from invaders.

As the game's main visualizer I did character design, story and animation, collaborating with a programmer on development.

Story

One day pigeons invaded the turtledoves' territory, using sheer numbers to dominate, bully and steal food. Outnumbered, the turtledoves made a final stand — sending their strongest warrior to reclaim their rightful home.
